Truffle Hunting & Val d'Orcia Afternoon
Tuscany · Montepulciano · Monticchiello
  • 🐕Morning: Truffle Hunting — 2.5 hrs with a truffle hunter and trained dog through the Montepulciano countryside. Arrange through Villa Poggiano. €50/person (€300 for group of 6).
  • 🍽️Lunch in Montepulciano — pair your truffle finds with local wine. Options to review:
    • 1. La Grotta — Michelin-recommended, 1500s building facing the Temple of San Biagio, truffle pasta a specialty. Reservations essential. Via di S. Biagio, 15
    • 2. Rosso Rubino Trattoria — small, intimate, outstanding quality pasta and meat dishes, fairly priced, good local wine list. Via Opio nel Corso, 34
    • 3. Osteria del Conte — pici pasta, T-bone steak, friendly staff, outdoor seating. Good for a group of 6. Main street
    • 4. La Bottega del Nobile — exposed-stone cellar, creative Tuscan dishes, terrace on the busiest corner of town. Via di Gracciano nel Corso, 95
    • 5. Trattoria di Cagnano — rustic, homemade pici pasta, shaded terrace, relaxed pace and very reasonable prices. Via di Cagnano
  • 🏰Afternoon: Monticchiello — tiny medieval fortified village 10 min from Pienza, far fewer tourists than the main Val d'Orcia towns; intact walls, a 13th-century church, and stunning valley views
  • 🌅Evening: Sunset wine in Montepulciano — back to the hotel or stop at a wine bar on Piazza Grande for Vino Nobile as the sun goes down over the valley

Bosnia Day Trip — Mostar & Kravice Falls Ideas Added
Bosnia & Herzegovina · Mostar · Kravice Falls
  • 🌉Mostar, Bosnia — UNESCO-listed Stari Most (Old Bridge), Turkish coffee in the bazaar, East-meets-West Balkan culture. ~2 hrs from Split.
  • 💧Kravice Waterfalls — dramatic 25-meter horseshoe falls into emerald pools; swimming allowed. Often described as "better than Krka, for less money."
Option A: Mostar + Kravice Falls Day Trip (Recommended)

This combo is consistently rated one of the best day trips from Split — a genuinely different cultural experience crossing into Bosnia. Guided group tours run ~€45–60/person all-in, with an English-speaking guide handling border crossings. Bring cash (EUR) for Bosnia.

Book via Viator →

Option C: Kravice Falls + Split Old Town or Beach Day

Skip Mostar entirely — drive to Kravice Falls in the morning (self-drive, ~1.5 hrs from Split), then head back and spend the afternoon in Split's Diocletian's Palace old town for a wander, lunch, and drinks — or simply back to the hotel beach at Le Méridien Lav for a relaxed afternoon. Good option if the group wants a lighter day before the Brač ferry.

Brač Island — Full Day Loop
Croatia · Brač Island
  • ⛴️Ferry Split → Supetar — departs 7:30 AM, ~1 hr crossing. Take rental car on the car ferry. Buy tickets at jadrolinija.hr — arrive 30–60 min early
  • ☕Supetar — arrive, grab coffee on the seafront
  • 🌲Nerežišća — former island capital with Baroque church (c.1750); spot the famous 150-year-old pine bonsai tree growing from the roof of the small square chapel
  • 🏔️Vidova Gora — 15-min drive up to the highest peak in all Croatian islands; panoramic views over Hvar and the Adriatic
  • 🏖️Zlatni Rat (Golden Horn), Bol — Croatia's most iconic beach; swim, sunbathe, grab a cocktail. Lunch in Bol town.
  • 🏘️Pučišća — 30-min drive from Bol; stunning white-stone seafront village built from the famous Brač stone (the same stone used for Diocletian's Palace and the White House)
  • 🏺Škrip — oldest settlement on the island (~3,000 years), known as "Croatian Mycenae"; traces of ancient Greeks, Illyrians, and Romans in a few square meters. Museum of Brač Island.
  • 🍽️Dinner in Splitska — coastal village at the foot of Škrip; historically the port for shipping Brač stone to build Diocletian's Palace. Seafood by the sea to end the day.
  • 🔗Full route reference: myislandbrac.com →
  • ⛴️Return ferry Supetar → Split — evening crossing
